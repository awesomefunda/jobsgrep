How it works

1

Download your role

Pick the sheet for your role family below. Each one has a Level column so you can filter by seniority in Excel.

2

Paste it into your AI

Open ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini. Paste the job rows plus your resume, and use the ranking prompt (one click to copy).

3

Get a ranked shortlist

Your AI scores every job against your experience and hands back a ranked list with reasons, gaps, and tailored hooks.

No accounts. No tracking your resume — ranking happens on your machine, with your AI. JobsGrep only ships the data.
